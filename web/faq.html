Help & FAQ

Can't find your answer? Contact us — we usually reply within one business day.

Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen

Click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for new apps that are not widely known yet. The app is safe, and every update it receives is digitally signed by us.

Where do I find the app after installing it?

Open the Start menu and type HTL Content Guard PC. It opens in its own window.

How long is the free trial, and what happens after it ends?

Every computer gets a free 4-day trial. When it ends, all of your existing blocks keep working. You only need Premium to add new blocks or make them stronger.

How do I buy Premium?

Open the app's Premium page and choose a plan.

  • In Bangladesh: pay with bKash or Nagad (Send Money to the number shown), enter the transaction ID (TrxID) from your payment message, and click Submit. Premium turns on automatically once the payment is confirmed, usually within a few hours.
  • Other countries: card and PayPal checkout is coming soon. Until then, email us with your Device ID and we'll help you pay.
I paid, but Premium isn't active yet

If the Premium page shows "Checking," please wait a little longer. If it has been more than 24 hours, contact us with your transaction ID and the Device ID shown at the bottom of the Premium page.

Why can't I uninstall the app?

You turned on Protection yourself, so the app can't be removed in a weak moment. Go to the app's Protection page and click Request uninstall. When the waiting time you chose (or the typing task) is complete, uninstall it from Windows Settings → Apps. Every protection level always keeps a safe way out.

Why do I need the browser extension?

Entire websites are blocked even without it. The HTL Content Guard PC extension adds blocking for Shorts and Reels, keyword filtering, and full SafeSearch. It installs itself in Chrome, Brave, Vivaldi and Chromium when you install the app. In Edge and Firefox, add it from the store.

Does my browsing history leave my computer?

No. The sites you visit never leave your computer. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

Can I move Premium to a new computer?

Premium is for one computer. If you switch computers, contact us with your old and new Device IDs and we'll move it for you.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Bengali, Arabic, Urdu, Indonesian, and Turkish. You can change the language any time in the app's Settings.
