Refund Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Try it free first

Every computer gets a free trial before you pay, so you can check that HTL Content Guard PC works for you.

14-day money-back guarantee

If Premium does not work for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your payment and we will refund the full amount. No long questions — just tell us your Device ID (shown on the app's Premium page) and how you paid.

How refunds are paid

  • Card / PayPal (Paddle): refunded by Paddle to the same card or PayPal account, usually within 5–10 working days depending on your bank.
  • bKash / Nagad / Rocket: sent back to the number you paid from, usually within 3 working days.

After a refund

Premium is removed from that computer. Blocks you already set keep working.

Renewals

Monthly and yearly plans do not renew by themselves when paid by bKash, Nagad or Rocket. You can ask for a refund of a renewal within 14 days of that payment, the same way.
