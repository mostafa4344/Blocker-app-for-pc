## Who this is for
FamilyGuard is a parental-control service. A **parent or guardian** creates an
account and manages the device of **their own minor child**. It is not for
monitoring adults and must not be used that way.

## What we collect

### From the parent (website account)
- Email address and authentication data (to sign in).
- Family and child profiles you create (child's display name, optional birth year).

### From the child's device (the app)
- **App usage:** which apps are used and for how long (aggregate foreground time).
- **Device status:** on/offline, battery level, current foreground app, OS/model.
- **Location:** only if you, the parent, turn on location sharing. Coarse points,
  short retention.
- **Device identifiers:** an internal device id we generate (not advertising IDs).

### What we do NOT collect — ever
- The **content** of messages, chats, emails, or calls.
- Call audio, ambient microphone audio, or camera capture.
- Keystrokes or screenshots of other apps.
- Advertising identifiers from the child device. The child app contains no ads
  and no third-party ad/analytics SDKs that transmit child identifiers.

## How the child is informed
The child's device always shows a persistent notification that protection is
active, the app icon is always visible, and a disclosure screen is shown during
setup listing exactly what is shared. Transparency is built into the product.

## How we use data
- To provide the service: enforce the limits you set, show you status and reports.
- To keep the service secure and working.
We do **not** sell personal data or use it for advertising.

## Legal basis / consent
The parent provides verifiable consent as the child's guardian and is the point
of control for the child's data (access, correction, deletion).

## Retention
- Usage data is aggregated to summaries and raw events are deleted after a short
  window.
- Location points are kept only for a short rolling window, then deleted.
- When you delete a child, device, or your account, associated data is deleted.

## Sharing
Data is processed by our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and push provider
(Firebase Cloud Messaging) solely to run the service. No other sharing.

## Your rights
Access, correct, export, or delete data; delete the account. Contact us at the
email above. We respond within the period your law requires.

## Children's privacy
The service is designed for a parent to manage a minor's device with consent and
data minimization, consistent with COPPA and GDPR-K principles. We do not create
independent accounts for children.

## Changes
We will post changes here and update the effective date; material changes will
be notified in-app/by email.
